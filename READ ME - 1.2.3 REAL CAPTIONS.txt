NTV FORGE 1.2.3 — REAL CAPTIONS BETA

This build adds a second subtitle-test route for real English captions on
INDIVIDUAL YouTube videos. The previous /subtitle-test/ route is unchanged.

WHAT TO TEST
1. Build and launch Forge as usual. Copy your existing tools folder first.
2. Pick a single YouTube video channel that has English captions, NOT a playlist.
3. Start its normal video stream in NTV to initialize HLS.
4. Create a separate temporary NTV live channel with this URL:
   http://YOUR-FORGE-IP:8765/subtitle-live/YOUR-CHANNEL-ID/master.m3u8
5. Play it and select Subtitles > English (Forge).
6. Compare displayed captions with the video audio.

NOTES
- The first request may take time while yt-dlp retrieves the caption file.
- If a video has no English captions, this test URL returns 404 rather than
  advertising a subtitle track that cannot be populated.
- Only single YouTube video sources are supported in this beta.
- Caption timing is an initial approximation using HLS segment numbers and
  the four-second segment target. Some video/keyframe combinations may drift.
- The source video's subtitles may be human-authored or auto-generated.
- No subtitle burning, changes to the Quick Sync pipeline, or extra video
  encoding are introduced.
- This is a test, not a production-ready caption synchronizer.
- Keep your 1.2.2 build as a fallback.
